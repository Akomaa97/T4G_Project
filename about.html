About Us

Built by the community, for its children.

The Love Project began as a single foster home and has grown into a full campus of care, classrooms, and clinics.

Our Story

In 2013, founder Grace Owusu opened her home to six children who had nowhere else to go. What started as informal fostering became a registered charity within two years, driven by one belief: children thrive when care and education are never separated.

Today, The Love Project houses and educates over 180 children across a residential campus, day school, and community outreach program.

Mission

To give every child a safe home, a quality education, and a path to independence.

Vision

A community where no child's future is limited by circumstance.
