Give your time. Change a trajectory.

From teaching a weekly class to offering professional skills, there's a role for almost anyone ready to show up consistently.

Classroom Teacher

Lead or assist lessons in literacy, math, or the arts.

Commitment: 3+ months, weekly

Mentor & Tutor

One-on-one academic or life-skills mentoring for teens.

Commitment: 6+ months, weekly

Skilled Professional

Medical, legal, or counseling support on a flexible schedule.

Commitment: Flexible

Requirements
  • Minimum age 18 (16 with guardian consent for supervised roles)
  • Background check and safeguarding orientation
  • Minimum 3-month commitment for on-campus roles

FAQs
Do I need experience working with children?

No — we provide orientation and ongoing support for every role.

Can I volunteer remotely?

Some roles, like tutoring or professional consulting, can be done virtually.

Is there a minimum time commitment?

Yes — consistency matters most to the children, so most roles ask for 3+ months.
